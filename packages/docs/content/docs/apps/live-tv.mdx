Live TV puts the channels from every addon in your configuration on one page, grouped, numbered and searchable, with a guide of what's on. It's in every AIOStreams app: in a browser, on computers, phones and tablets, and on TVs.

![Live TV on a computer: the programme guide, with the playing channel in a preview above it and its programme's details beside it](/apps/live-tv/guide.webp)

## Adding channels

Channels come from addons, like everything else in AIOStreams. Add one in the configure page's addon marketplace:

- **Your IPTV provider.** The **Xtream** addon signs in with the server address, username and password your provider gave you. It brings the provider's channels, their groups and their guide, and earlier programmes where the provider keeps them.
- **Free channels**, from marketplace addons such as **USA TV** or **Debridio TV**.
- **Any Stremio addon with channels**, added by its URL, such as one that reads an M3U playlist and an XMLTV guide.

Once your configuration has one, **Live TV** shows in the navigation. The same channels show in Stremio, and the guide fills Stremio's guide too. Making an addon? See [Live TV in the addon protocol](/reference/addon-protocol/live-tv).

<Callout type="info">
  Hiding, renaming and reordering channels is up to the addon. The Xtream
  addon keeps your provider's order and groups, and can hide some of them, as
  below.
</Callout>

### Hiding groups and channels from an Xtream login

The Xtream addon has four options, in its advanced settings, that take comma-separated keywords. A keyword matches anywhere in a name and ignores case.

| Option               | Does                                                        |
| -------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Exclude Groups**   | Hides groups matching a keyword, and channels only in them. |
| **Include Groups**   | Shows only groups matching a keyword.                       |
| **Exclude Channels** | Hides channels matching a keyword.                          |
| **Include Channels** | Shows only channels matching a keyword.                     |

An exclude always wins over an include, and a channel in several groups stays while one of them does.

Some examples:

- **Hide pay-per-view and adult groups.** Exclude Groups: `PPV, ADULT` hides groups such as `US | PPV Events` and `XXX Adult`.
- **Keep only UK and Irish channels.** Include Groups: `UK, IRE` shows groups such as `UK | News` and `IRE | Sports`, and nothing else.
- **Drop radio and test channels.** Exclude Channels: `RADIO, TEST` hides `BBC Radio 4` and `Test Card`.
- **Only a few broadcasters.** Include Channels: `BBC, ITV` shows channels such as `BBC One HD` and `ITV2`.
- **Combine them.** Include Groups: `UK` with Exclude Channels: `Shopping` shows the UK groups without their shopping channels.

Whoever runs the instance can set defaults for addons that leave an option empty, with the `BUILTIN_XTREAM_EXCLUDE_GROUPS`, `BUILTIN_XTREAM_INCLUDE_GROUPS`, `BUILTIN_XTREAM_EXCLUDE_CHANNELS` and `BUILTIN_XTREAM_INCLUDE_CHANNELS` [environment variables](/configuration/environment-variables). For example, `BUILTIN_XTREAM_EXCLUDE_GROUPS=PPV,ADULT` hides those groups for everyone. An addon that sets its own keywords uses them instead.

Channels you hide can't be played either, as they're no longer in the list.

## Finding a channel

- **Groups** run along the top: **Favourites** and **Recent** first, then **All channels** and the groups your addons send. With many groups, a button opens them in a list you can search.
- **Guide** and **Channels** switch between the programme guide and a plain list, on screens wide enough for a guide and when your channels have programmes. Phones and TVs show the list.
- **Days** step through the guide: **Today**, **Tomorrow**, and earlier days for channels with [catch-up](#catch-up).
- **Channel numbers**: type a number on a keyboard or remote to go to that channel. Channels keep the numbers their addon gives them, or are numbered in order.
- **Search** finds channels by name or number, next to films and shows.

The page reopens on the channel you were last on.

## Watching

Pick a channel and it plays in a preview at the top of the page, so you can keep looking through the guide while it plays. **Full screen** opens the player, and **Back** returns to the guide with the preview still playing.

In the player:

- **Up** and **Down** on a remote, **Page Up** and **Page Down** on a keyboard, or a remote's channel buttons go to the next or previous channel. Type a number to jump to one, and **Previous channel** goes back to the last.
- The bar shows the programme on now and how far into it you are. Pause and you fall behind live; **Go live** catches up.
- **Channels** opens the lineup without leaving the player.
- A channel that drops is reopened by itself. If it keeps failing, the app moves on to the channel's next source.

Live channels start without subtitles. Audio and subtitle tracks you pick are kept for that channel.

## Programmes

Select a programme in the guide for its details:

- **Watch** on the programme on now, and **From the start** to play it from its beginning where the channel has [catch-up](#catch-up).
- **Remind me** on a later one. While the app is open, it asks at the programme's start whether to switch to it. Reminders are kept on that device, for each user.

## Catch-up

Some providers keep the last few days of a channel's programmes. Those channels show earlier days in the guide, and their past programmes play from the start, as a recording you can pause and seek. Through the Xtream addon, catch-up comes from your provider's archive.

## Favourites

**Add favourite** in a channel's details puts it in the **Favourites** group, on the **Favourites** page under **Channels**, and in a row on **Home**. To open the app on Live TV, set **Settings → Interface → Start on**.
