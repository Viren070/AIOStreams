AIOStreams brings your Stremio addons, debrid services and Usenet together into one list, filtered, sorted and formatted your way, and plays it in Stremio or in its own apps for every screen.

You configure it once. When you open a title, it asks every source you've added, removes the duplicates, filters and sorts what comes back by your rules, and names each result with your format. Stremio gets that list through the addon; the AIOStreams app and Jellyfin apps get the same list as versions of the title.

## The ideas behind it

| Idea | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Instance** | An AIOStreams server: a [public one](/getting-started/public-instances), [your own](/getting-started/deployment), or ElfHosted. |
| **Configuration** | Your setup on an instance, saved with a password. You open it with its UUID and password, or from a [profile](/guides/config-profiles) when you sign in. |
| **Sources** | Where results come from: Stremio addons from the marketplace or by URL, AIOStreams' built-in addons, and your debrid and Usenet services, which you enter once for every addon. |
| **Rules** | What every result goes through: deduplication, [filters](/configuration/options), sorting (including [scored sorting](/guides/scored-sorting)), and the [formatter](/reference/custom-formatter). [Groups](/guides/groups) and [variants](/reference/config-expressions) decide which sources are asked and how. |
| **Where you watch** | Stremio, through the addon; the [AIOStreams app](/apps) and [Jellyfin apps](/apps/jellyfin), through your configuration's [media server](/apps/media-server); and Seanime, Chillio or Aniyomi. |

## On your own instance

Everything above works on a public instance. Hosting AIOStreams yourself adds:

- the [built-in Usenet engine](/guides/usenet#the-built-in-engine-aiostreams), which streams straight from your NNTP provider, archives included;
- [Sonarr and Radarr](/guides/sonarr-radarr) support, with the engine as their download client and AIOStreams as their indexer;
- the [release blocklist](/guides/release-blocklist), shared between instances if you like;
- the dashboard, with live streams, analytics, logs, users and every runtime setting, and [SSO](/guides/sso) for signing in to it.
