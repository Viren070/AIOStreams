The latest AIOStreams TV apps. See the [smart TV guide](https://aiostreams.org/apps/smart-tv) for how to install them.

### LG (webOS)

[aiostreams-webos.ipk](@BASE@/aiostreams-webos.ipk) (1080p), for Developer Mode. On a TV that
struggles at that resolution, [aiostreams-webos-720p.ipk](@BASE@/aiostreams-webos-720p.ipk) draws a
smaller canvas instead. With the Homebrew Channel, add `@REPOSITORY@` as a repository in its
settings instead: the app then installs and updates from the TV. The channel installs the 1080p
package.

### Samsung (Tizen)

[aiostreams-tizen.wgt](@BASE@/aiostreams-tizen.wgt), unsigned: the installer signs it for your TV.
